### Strict Eligibility Criteria:
- **Institution & Regulation**: MLRIT Autonomous **R22** strictly.
- **Examination Type**: **Regular** examinations only (Supplementary / supply papers strictly excluded).
- **Examination Years**: **2024** and **2025** strictly (2021, 2022, 2023 excluded).
- **Maximum Marks**: **60 Marks** strictly (70M legacy papers excluded).
- **Mark Structure**: Part A (10 × 1M = 10 Marks) + Part B (5 units × 10M = 50 Marks, composed of direct 10M questions or 5M + 5M subquestions). Evaluated total = exactly 60 marks.
- **Idempotency**: Question deduplication guaranteed via SHA-256 hash checking.
